Privacy Policy

rclone-drive is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google user data is accessed only to upload, list and organize the owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, sold or shared with any third party.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own machine.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
